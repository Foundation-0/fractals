# Repeated patterns, records and changing scales

Research note, 12 November 2026. This note connects the Witness Map, collective waves and turbulence to the Foundation0 convergence study. It records model calculations and proposed tests. It reports no new observation or universal microscopic mechanism.

## Interference loss, readable records and feedback

The convergence probes construct environments with the same reduced system state and the same complete dephasing channel, which removes interference. In one example a single environment qubit reveals the pointer bit. In another, every one- or two-qubit fragment is uninformative because the information resides in joint correlations. The latter uses an established error-correcting code.

This separates three questions for the Witness Map: how fast interference disappears, which fragments an observer can read, and whether the environment later affects the system. A departing photon can carry a record without returning influence. A history-dependent equation can describe returning influence without establishing a readable record.

[Riedel and Zurek, Figure 2(b) and equations 17–18](https://arxiv.org/html/1001.3419v2) supply a physical limiting case: ideal isotropic illumination can cause decoherence without creating redundant readable records. The comparison does not invalidate a stated decoherence rate. It limits what that rate alone establishes.

## Predict the movement of a boundary

The turbulent viscous scale obeys η = (ν³/ε)¹⁄⁴ under the usual incompressible-flow assumptions. Increasing viscosity ν by 100 at fixed dissipation rate ε moves the scale by 31.62.

The point of comparison is this predicted response, rather than a coincident length or fitted slope. [Kolmogorov's primary scaling paper](https://www.ams.jhu.edu/~eyink/Turbulence/classics/Kolmogorov41a.pdf) gives the underlying construction.

A sonic transition is another boundary, with different controls. [Federrath et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2011.06238) find a transition between supersonic and subsonic scaling, rather than the end of all turbulent structure. Equilibrium Monte Carlo update counts also cannot serve as a physical quench clock without a dynamical model.

Collective wave behavior needs its own description. A single particle's witness number does not specify the persistence or energy transport of a classical field pattern. [Mossman et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/1712.06689) connect shock behavior in a driven atomic condensate with three-dimensional wave dynamics and transverse instabilities.

That atomic-gas result does not establish a dark-field pressure floor or AeST dynamics.

## Next tests

- In one stated photon-scattering model, compute interference loss, accessible information in environment fragments and later system response separately. Vary illumination anisotropy while keeping the comparison's other inputs fixed.
- Change viscosity or forcing independently and predict the cutoff response before fitting it. Keep cloud contour definitions, resolution and projection fixed when comparing shapes.
- For a collective collapse calculation, track resolved energy, smaller excitations and boundary fluxes. Compare one and three dimensions before interpreting retained velocity dispersion as pressure or thermalization.
